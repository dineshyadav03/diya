---
name: weekly-review
description: Use when I ask for a weekly review, how my week went, or what I should focus on next week
triggers: [user]
argument-hint: nothing needed
---
Look only at what I have told you this week (reminders done, tasks done, things I said). Say three things: what got done, what slipped, and one thing to focus on next week. Do not invent anything that is not in what you were given; if there is little, say so.
